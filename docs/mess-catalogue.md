# Mess catalogue

Generated by `scripts/mess_catalogue.py` from the mess registry. Do not edit by hand.
Examples come from the `nightmare` preset with 300 households and seed 42.

A mess type's *intensity* (0 to 1) times its *max rate* is the share of eligible rows it affects. Types run in this order: `households`, `names`, `formats`, `balances`.
